Neural imaging

Following synapses as memories form.

An image-analysis pipeline for longitudinal synaptic structural plasticity.

The University of Hong Kong · Caro Lai’s groupApr–Nov 2025

The question

How can bouton and dendritic spine formation and elimination be quantified across longitudinal in vivo two-photon imaging sessions to study structural plasticity during memory formation?

From imaging sessions to tracked structures

During a remote research internship in Caro Lai’s group at the University of Hong Kong, I processed two-photon imaging data in ImageJ, manually tracked perisomatic boutons and dendritic spines, and quantified their formation and elimination.

Figure forthcomingTwo-photon images, detections, and longitudinal tracks

My contributions

I developed an automated 4D analysis pipeline to support manual annotation. It combines Cascade R-CNN-based two-dimensional detection, three-dimensional volumetric reconstruction, and longitudinal tracking across imaging sessions.

Outcome

I delivered the algorithm to laboratory members for routine image annotation and analysis.

Example imaging data and analysis figures will be added here to illustrate detection, reconstruction, and tracking across sessions.
